# Establish the capability

## Goal

Establish what the skill must achieve, when it applies, and the requirements and information that govern its execution.

## Procedure

Identify the intended outcome, conditions of use, inputs, prerequisites, and boundaries from the user's request.

Locate and inspect relevant sources:

- **Project context:** instructions, documentation, existing procedures, and related skills.
- **Prior work:** supplied examples, previous work, and evidence of actual execution.
- **Execution dependencies:** scripts, tools, and platform contracts that the procedure depends on.

Distinguish established requirements from proposals and illustrative material. Resolve conflicting guidance against its authority, scope, and applicability.

For a scoped revision, preserve established behavior outside the requested change.

Provide the established capability, relevant sources, constraints, and unresolved requirements to the lead agent. Identify missing information that prevents a correct decision.
