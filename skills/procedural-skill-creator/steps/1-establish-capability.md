# Establish the capability

## Goal

Establish what the skill must achieve, when it applies, and the requirements and information that govern its execution.

## Procedure

Start from the user's request and inspect relevant sources:

- **Project context:** instructions, documentation, existing procedures, and related skills.
- **Prior work:** supplied examples, previous work, and evidence of actual execution.
- **Execution dependencies:** scripts, tools, and platform contracts that the procedure depends on.

### Questions to explore

Use relevant questions to investigate the capability, including questions not listed here.

- **Outcome**
  - What should be different after successful execution?
  - What would a satisfactory result contain?
- **Scope**
  - Which requests and situations require this capability?
  - Which similar requests need a different capability or no skill? What distinguishes them?
  - Which responsibilities belong to or are shared with related skills and procedures?
- **Knowledge**
  - What information, prerequisites, and constraints are available? What is missing?
  - Which decisions require project knowledge, domain expertise, or user preferences?
- **Uncertainty**
  - What is explicitly established and what is inferred?
  - Which assumptions or unresolved choices could materially change the result?

### Interpret the sources

Distinguish what each source contributes:

- **Requirements:** conditions execution must satisfy.
- **Existing procedures:** established behavior, prerequisites, and decisions.
- **Decisions:** accepted choices, rationale, and applicable conditions.
- **Observations:** demonstrated behavior and limits of the evidence.
- **Examples:** intended illustrations and incidental details.
- **User corrections:** earlier interpretations or choices that no longer apply.
- **Proposals:** possibilities that remain undecided.

Account for other relevant contributions. Resolve conflicting guidance against its authority, scope, applicability, and supporting evidence. For a scoped revision, preserve established behavior outside the requested change.

Provide the established capability, selection boundaries, relevant sources, constraints, and unresolved requirements to the lead agent. Identify missing information that prevents a correct decision.
