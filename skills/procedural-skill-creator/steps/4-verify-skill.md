# Verify the skill

## Goal

Independently establish whether the skill's instructions and resources are consistent with its intended outcome and designed procedure.

## Procedure

Review the skill against the established capability and procedure.

### Red flags

Investigate relevant warning signs before treating them as defects:

- Actions described as activities without an identifiable result.
- Dependent actions assuming unavailable results or a successful preceding step.
- Parallel actions with conflicting changes or unresolved dependencies.
- Decisions lacking criteria that distinguish materially different actions.
- Delegated work with overlapping or unclear responsibilities.
- Repeated instructions or restrictions on choices that do not affect the result.
- Simplified instructions that omit consequential conditions or exceptions.

### Verification

Confirm:

- [ ] The name and description distinguish intended requests from relevant near-misses.
- [ ] Each action has the necessary inputs, and its possible results lead to a valid next action or completion.
- [ ] Required decisions, constraints, exceptions, and unresolved cases remain covered.
- [ ] The skill contains only guidance needed to execute the procedure.
- [ ] Metadata, resources, and required helpers are usable.

For orchestrated skills, check that responsibilities, execution conditions, dependencies, and expected results are sufficient to coordinate the steps.

Report completed checks, findings and the affected steps, and unresolved problems to the lead agent. Identify any questions that require actual execution to resolve.
